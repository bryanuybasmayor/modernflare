01Our Story

38 Years of Excellence

A Melfort tradition, refined.

Modern Flare Hair Design has been part of Melfort for more than 38 years. In that time we’ve styled generations of families, celebrated weddings, first days of school and fresh starts — and we’re still on the same stretch of Main Street we started on.

Thank you to the Melfort community for trusting us with your hair year after year. Our 4.6 out of 5 client rating belongs to you as much as it does to us: it’s built on consistency, care, and taking the time to get the details right.

03Professional Retail

Salon results, every day at home.

The right products make a good haircut last. We stock the professional lines our stylists use in the chair, so you can keep your colour rich and your hair healthy between visits — and we’ll show you exactly how to use them.

  • Redken Colour care & styling

04Our Team

The hands behind the flare.

Our team is made up of experienced stylists and a registered massage therapist who have chosen to build their careers in Melfort. Between them they cover women’s and men’s cutting, colour, styling and therapeutic massage — and they keep training so their techniques stay current.

You’ll be looked after by the same faces visit after visit, which is exactly how a 38-year-old salon should feel.
